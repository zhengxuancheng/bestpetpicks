Bringing home a bearded dragon or leopard gecko is the fun part. Feeding them right is where most new owners get confused. Live insects need housing and gut-loading. Powdered diets need mixing ratios. Calcium without D3 is useless — calcium with too much D3 is dangerous.

We tested over 30 reptile foods, feeder insect setups, and supplement powders across six months with help from three experienced reptile keepers. These seven products earned a permanent spot in our feeding rotation.

## What to Look For in Reptile Food and Feeding Supplies

Not all reptile foods are created equal. A crested gecko diet wont work for a bearded dragon, and so on. We looked at four things when testing these products.

**Complete nutrition.** For powdered diets and canned foods — the ingredient list should match the species natural diet. Bearded dragons need more plant matter. Leopard geckos need more protein. Snakes need whole prey.

**Supplement quality.** Calcium powder is the most important supplement you will buy. It must contain vitamin D3 for most indoor reptiles or have a D3-free option for reptiles with UVB lighting. Phosphorus-free calcium is preferable.

**Feeder insect viability.** If you are buying live insects, they need to arrive alive and stay alive for at least a week. Gut-load potential matters too. Crickets that you feed well become better food for your pet.

**Convenience and shelf life.** Freeze-dried and canned options last months on the shelf. Live insects need weekly trips to the pet store unless you start a colony. Pick what fits your schedule.

## FAQ

### How often should I feed my bearded dragon?

Adult bearded dragons should eat once a day with salads available and insects every other day. Juveniles need insects twice daily. The amount varies by size but a good rule is as many insects as they can eat in 10 minutes.

### Do I need to gut-load feeder insects?

Yes. Feeder insects are only as nutritious as what they have eaten in the last 24 hours. Feed them commercial gut-load diet or fresh vegetables for at least 12 hours before feeding them to your reptile. This makes a measurable difference in calcium intake.

### Can I feed my leopard gecko only powdered diet?

Some leopard geckos will accept Repashy Grub Pie as a staple but most need live insects to thrive. Powdered diets work best as a supplement for days you cannot get to the pet store or as part of a rotation with live feeders.

### What is the difference between calcium with D3 and without D3?

Reptiles use UVB light to produce vitamin D3 naturally, which allows them to absorb calcium. If your enclosure has proper UVB, use calcium without D3 to avoid overdose. If your reptile is indoors without UVB, use calcium with D3. Using both is dangerous.

### How long do dubia roaches live in a bin?

With proper care — egg crates for hiding, a moisture source like a potato slice, and temperatures between 70 and 90 degrees Fahrenheit — dubia roaches stay alive for 6 to 8 weeks. They reproduce slowly at room temperature so you do not need to worry about infestations.
