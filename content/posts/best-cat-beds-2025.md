## FAQ

### Why won't my cat use the bed I bought?

Cats choose sleeping spots based on warmth, security, and height. Try placing the bed in a spot your cat already sleeps, sprinkle catnip on it, or put a worn t-shirt inside for your scent. Some cats need days or weeks to accept a new bed — don't return it after 24 hours.

### Should I get a heated bed?

If your home drops below 65°F in winter, or your cat is over 10 years old, yes. The K&H Thermo-Kitty's 4W pad is safe, energy-efficient, and genuinely improves quality of life for cold cats. Unplug when not in use and check the cord regularly for chew marks.
