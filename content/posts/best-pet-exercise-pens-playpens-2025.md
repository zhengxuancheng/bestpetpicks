An exercise pen (or "ex-pen" as most owners call them) is one of those pet products you don't realize you need until you have a hyper puppy, a small dog that needs supervised outdoor time, or a rabbit that deserves more than a cage. It's a portable enclosed space that gives your pet room to move without the full freedom of a dog-proofed house or the confinement of a crate.

We tested 6 exercise pens across different use cases. Wire panels for heavy-duty containment, portable fabric pens for travel, low-height pens for small animals, and extra-tall options for jumpers. Here is what we found.

## What to Look For in an Exercise Pen

### Panel Height: How High Does Your Pet Jump?

Height is the most important dimension. For small dogs and puppies under 15 pounds, 24 inches is usually enough. For medium dogs and most jumpers, 30 inches is the minimum. For agile breeds like Jack Russell Terriers, border collies, and huskies, you want 36 to 48 inches. Small animals like rabbits and guinea pigs can clear 18 inches if they try, so 24 inches is a safe bet for them too.

### Panel Count and Configuration

Exercise pens typically come in 4, 6, or 8-panel configurations. A 6-panel pen creates a roughly 24-square-foot enclosure (around 4 feet in diameter for a round setup). An 8-panel gives you 36 square feet. Most pens can be configured in a circle, oval, rectangle, or L-shape. Some can also be used as room dividers or attached to a crate opening to create a crate-pen combo.

### Material and Durability

Wire pens with welded construction are the strongest. Look for pens with horizontal and vertical crossbars on each panel — horizontal bars are climbable by determined dogs, so vertical-only or alternating designs are safer. For small animals, look for narrow bar spacing (under 1 inch) to prevent escape. Fabric and mesh pens are lightweight and portable but will not stop a chewer or a determined escape artist.

### Portability and Foldability

If you plan to take the pen to the park, camping, or to a friend's house, look for pens that fold flat or into a compact carry case. Wire pens with a "fold-flat" hinge design collapse into a thin stack of panels. Fabric pens fold into a circular disc that fits in a carrying bag. Heavy-duty outdoor pens are not portable.

### Floor and Ground Compatibility

Exercise pens do not come with floors. You need to place them on a non-slip surface — grass, a patio, a rug, or a vinyl mat. On hardwood or tile, the pen will slide unless you weight the bottom or use rubber grip strips on the panel feet. Some pens include ground stakes for outdoor use.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between an exercise pen and a crate?

An exercise pen is an open-top enclosure with no ceiling. It gives the pet room to walk, run small circles, play with toys, and interact with people while staying contained. A crate is a fully enclosed space with a top, designed as a den-like resting area. They serve different purposes: the pen is for active time and supervised play; the crate is for naps, quiet time, and house training. Many owners use both — a crate for sleeping and a pen for daytime confinement when they cannot supervise directly.

### How tall should my exercise pen be?

Measure your pet's jumping ability, not their standing height. Most dogs can jump 2-3 times their height at the shoulder. A 24-inch pen works for small breeds and puppies under 15 pounds. A 30-inch pen is the minimum for medium dogs and most jumpers. For agile breeds like huskies, border collies, and terriers, go with 36 inches. For giant breeds, pick 48 inches. Small animals like rabbits and guinea pigs can clear 18 inches, so 24 inches is safe for them.

### Can I use an exercise pen outdoors?

Yes, but with precautions. Wire pens on grass need ground stakes to prevent tipping. Fabric pens are not suitable for outdoor use on rough ground. Never leave a pet unsupervised in an outdoor pen — they can tip it, dig under it, or climb over it. Place the pen on level ground away from obstacles the pet could use to boost themselves over the top.

### How do I clean an exercise pen?

Wire pens: wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap. For urine or mud, use a hose and let it dry fully before storing. Fabric pens: remove the floor mat and wash it separately. Spot-clean the mesh walls with a cloth and mild soap. Do not machine-dry fabric pens — air dry only to prevent shrinking. Store dry to prevent mildew.

### Can I connect an exercise pen to a crate?

Some pens are designed for this. The MidWest Foldable pen includes connector clips that attach to MidWest crates, creating a combined crate-and-pen setup. This is especially useful for house training — the crate becomes the sleeping area, and the pen becomes the daytime living space. Other brands may work together with zip ties or carabiners, but check compatibility before buying.
